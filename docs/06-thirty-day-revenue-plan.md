# 06 · Thirty-Day Revenue Plan

Owner: founder (growth/sales + ops). Governing decisions: D-004 (buyer priority), D-005/D-006
(fees & pricing), D-008 (poker seed listing), D-009 (metrics), D-010 (goal), D-017 (stunts).
Goal restated from D-010: **3–5 paid campaigns, ≥ $5,000 cash collected, ≥ $1,500
contribution margin, inside 30 days**, before the marketplace is built out. Everything below
is a plan, not a forecast; conversion rates are labeled estimates until real data replaces them.

## 1. The math we are betting on (estimates, per A-01/A-11)

| Funnel step | Target | Assumed conversion | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prospects on list | 100 | — | Built week 1 |
| Contacted (≥1 touch) | 100 | 100% | Founder-led |
| Positive reply / meeting | 20–30 | 20–30% est. | Hyper-personalized founder outbound; unproven |
| Discovery calls held | 18–25 | — | Overlaps the 20 buyer interviews |
| Briefs collected | 10–14 | ~50% of calls est. | |

| Verbal yes | 4–6 | ~40–50% of proposals est. | |
| **Paid** | **3–5** | ~75% of verbals | Payment upfront at booking (doc 18) |

Cash mix that clears $5,000 (illustrative): 2 managed campaigns at $1,500–$2,500 (D-006
minimum $1,500) + 2 seed-listing packages (D-008: $450 / $750 / $1,500 tiers, buyer pays +5%).
Example: $2,000 + $1,500 managed + $750 + $1,500 packages ≈ $5,750 charged + buyer fees on
package bookings. Contribution per D-008 economics runs ≈ $37–$166 on packages; managed
campaigns at 35–50% gross margin carry the $1,500 contribution target — so **at least 2 of the
3–5 paid campaigns must be managed campaigns**.

## 2. Week-by-week schedule

### Week 1 (days 1–7) — Build the machine, start talking
- **Prospect list:** 100 named prospects in `data/lead-list-template.csv` format by day 3.
  Split per D-004: 40 agencies (Denver + remote-buying agencies), 35 startups/DTC, 25 local
  Denver businesses. Sources: agency directories, Denver Startup Week alumni lists, LinkedIn,
  RiNo/LoDo storefront walks.
- **Interviews begin:** book 8 buyer interviews and 10 seller interviews (scripts in doc 18).
  Interviews are honest research, not disguised pitches — say so at the top of each call.
- **Catalog curation starts:** 10 candidate listings drafted from seller conversations
  (people, vehicles, spaces, event moments). Poker seed listing (D-008) finalized; contact
  2–3 Denver-area card rooms about venue approval (A-12 — do this before selling Tier 3).
- **Surfaces live by day 5:** landing page, buyer brief form, seller intake form (typeform-or
  -equivalent is fine; the app admin replaces it later). No fake traction anywhere (D-020).
- **Outbound starts day 3:** 10 new contacts/day once the list exists.
- **Templates written:** proposal, agreement, invoice, proof-of-completion checklist,
  case-study skeleton (structures in docs 07 and 18).

### Week 2 (days 8–14) — Interview velocity, first proposals
- Buyer interviews: cumulative 14. Seller interviews: cumulative 18.
- Catalog: 25 curated items minimum (A-05 gate).
- Outbound: 10 new contacts/day + follow-up ladder (§4). First 4–6 discovery calls.
- **First 3 proposals out** by day 12 (48h SLA from brief).
- Stunt prep: lock poker tournament date/venue; scope "Sponsor a Denver dog-walking day"
  permits/route (doc 19). Note: Denver park event permits require ~60-day lead time
  (see doc 19 §risk notes), so week-2 stunt planning favors private venues, sidewalks, and
  film-permit-eligible shoots (Denver film permits are free — citation in doc 19).
- **Day 10 checkpoint** (§7).

### Week 3 (days 15–21) — Close the first cash
- Target: **first 1–2 paid campaigns** (likely a local business or startup — fastest close per
  D-004 — even though agencies are priority #1 for repeat value; we work all three lanes).
- Buyer interviews: cumulative 18. Seller interviews: cumulative 22. Catalog: 35+.
- Proposals: cumulative 6–8. Referral offer goes live (§6) — mentioned at every verbal yes.
- Execute stunt #1 if booked (poker tournament is flagship and doubles as seed listing).
- Manual matching workflow in daily use (doc 07 §3): every brief matched to 2–3 catalog
  items within 24h, logged in the matching sheet.

### Week 4 (days 22–30) — Stack paid, execute, prove
- Target: cumulative **3–5 paid, ≥ $5,000 cash**. Interviews complete: 20 buyer / 25 seller.
- Catalog: 40–50. Execute first campaigns; collect proof to doc 07 standards same-day.
- First case study drafted within 72h of first completed campaign (template in §5).
- **Day 20 checkpoint** happened day 20; **day 30 checkpoint** closes the month (§7).

## 3. Daily sales operating cadence (Mon–Fri)

| Time | Block | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 8:00–8:30 | Pipeline review | Walk every open opportunity; anything stale per follow-up rules gets today's action; update ops log |
| 8:30–10:00 | Outbound block | **10 new contacts/day** (new = first touch), personalized; batch by segment |
| 10:00–12:30 | Calls | Interviews + discovery (target 2–3/day at peak) |
| 13:30–15:30 | Delivery/ops | Proposals (48h SLA), matching, pre-production, seller curation |
| 15:30–16:30 | Follow-ups | Every promised follow-up sent same day; no exceptions |
| 16:30–17:00 | Log + plan | Time tracking (doc 07 §8), tomorrow's 10 contacts pre-picked |

Follow-up rules: touch cadence day 0 / +3 / +7 / +14 then quarterly nurture; a lead is never
"lost" silently — it is marked lost with a reason code (no-budget / timing / fit / unresponsive).
Every call ends with a scheduled next step on the calendar or an explicit close-out.

## 4. Pipeline stages and exit criteria

| Stage | Exit criteria (advance when ALL true) |
|---|---|
| Lead | Fits ICP (segment, city, plausible $750+ budget); added to list with owner + next action |
| Contacted | ≥1 personalized touch delivered; no bounce |
| Discovery | Call held; budget band, timeline, decision maker, brand-safety constraints captured (doc 18 §6) |
| Brief | Written brief received via form or transcribed by us and confirmed by buyer |
| Proposal | Proposal sent within 48h of brief; priced per D-005/D-006; expiry date stated |
| Verbal | Buyer says yes to a specific package + price + date window |
| Paid | **Cash collected** — packages paid in full at booking; managed campaigns 50% deposit paid (doc 18 §10). Not "PO issued." Paid means money moved. |
| Live | Agreement signed, venue approval confirmed where required (D-016), execution scheduled |
| Completed | Deliverables done; proof package accepted by buyer; final 50% collected on managed campaigns |
| Case study | Written, buyer-approved for the public version (or logged internal-only), published |

## 6. Referral offer — recorded rule

**Give $250, get $250:** an existing buyer who refers a new buyer gets a $250 credit toward
their next campaign, and the referred buyer gets $250 off their first campaign — **both credits
release only when the referred campaign reaches Completed** (proof accepted, cash retained).
Credits apply to campaigns ≥ $1,500 so a credit never drops a transaction below the D-005
$250 minimum or guts package margin; one credit per campaign; no cash value. Rationale: at
$1,500+ AOV, $250 is a 10–17% incentive costing less than estimated CAC (A-11 ≤ $400).
Revisit at 10 completed campaigns.

## 8. Kill / pivot / continue checkpoints (summary — full gates live in `23-kill-pivot-continue-criteria.md`, owned by another teammate)

- **Day 10:** ≥50 contacts made, ≥10 buyer interviews, ≥1 proposal out, ≥15 catalog items.
  If reply rate < 5% after 50 personalized touches → pivot messaging/segment before adding
  volume. If sellers decline the 80/20 split at > 40% (A-03) → escalate pricing review.
- **Day 20:** ≥1 paid campaign OR ≥3 verbals. Zero verbals after 8+ proposals → pivot: change
  package structure or lead segment (e.g., go local-first for cash, agencies for pipeline).
  Willingness-to-pay signals from ≥14 interviews contradicting $750–$5k (A-01) → reprice.
- **Day 30:** D-010 met → continue (scale outbound, start doc 20 city criteria tracking).
  1–2 paid + strong pipeline → continue with a named fix and a day-45 re-gate. < 2 paid after
  ~100 qualified contacts (A-01 falsification) → stop and run the kill/pivot analysis in doc 23
  before spending another dollar on this motion.
