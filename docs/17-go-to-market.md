# 17 · Go-To-Market Strategy

Scope: how SponsorThis gets its first and next 50 buyers in Denver (D-003), consistent with
the managed-marketplace posture (D-001) and the 30-day plan (doc 06). All yields below are
**estimates with reasoning, not measurements** (D-020); no channel has been run yet.

## 1. Positioning statement

> **anything sponsorable** — real people, moments, objects, spaces, and permitted stunts —

One-liner: **"Make anything sponsorable."** Supporting: "Real people. Real moments.
Real-world attention."

## 2. The three demand channels, in order

### Channel 1 — Founder-led outbound (primary; months 1–3)

The whole doc 06/18 machine. Priority per D-004: agencies → startups/DTC → local Denver
businesses.

- **Cost:** founder time (~3h/day) + tools ~$150/mo (email, e-sign, forms, data). No paid media.
- **Expected yield (estimate):** 100 prospects/mo → 20–30% positive reply (personalized
  founder mail to warm-ish local network + cold) → 8–12 proposals → 3–5 paid. Implies CAC
  ≤ $400/paid campaign if founder time is costed at $40/hr (A-11); falsified if CAC > $1,000
  with < 20% proposal-win rate.
- **Why first:** only channel with day-1 revenue, and every call is also pricing research.

### Channel 2 — Launch stunts as PR/content engine (months 1–2, recurring)

The doc 19 engine; top three per D-017: poker-tournament sponsorship (flagship + seed
listing), Denver dog-walking day, 100 branded bike commutes.

- **Cost per stunt (estimate):** $200–$1,500 hard costs (permits where needed, merch,
  content capture) + 6–12h founder/contractor time. Poker stunt hard costs are minimal —
  the sponsor's package funds it (D-008 economics).
- **Expected yield (estimate):** each stunt → 1 paid campaign (it IS one), 20–60 content
  assets, 3–10 warm inbound conversations, 1 case study. Press is a bonus, not a plan —
  we pitch local outlets (Denverite, BusinessDen, 303 Magazine, Denver Post biz desk) with
  a real story, and we do not buy or fake coverage.
- **Rule (D-017):** at least one stunt becomes a public case study in the first 60 days.

### Channel 3 — Case-study-driven inbound + SEO groundwork (months 2–6; compounding)

- **Cost:** ~4h/case study + $0 media.
- **Assets:** one case-study page per completed campaign (real numbers only, buyer-approved);
  category pages seeded from the catalog ("sponsor a poker player," "sponsor a dog walker,"
  "sponsor a bike commuter," "sponsor a mural wall") targeting long-tail queries with
  near-zero competition (hypothesis: these queries have low volume but near-perfect intent —
  we accept low volume for high close rate; unverified).
- **Expected yield (estimate):** months 2–3: 1–3 inbound leads/mo; month 6: 5–15/mo if
  10+ case studies exist. Wide error bars; treated as free upside, never load-bearing in
  the 30-day goal.

## 3. The flywheel

```
stunt → content assets → case study → outbound proof points → more paid campaigns
   ↑                                                                  |
   └───────────── new sellers see coverage & apply ← ─────────────────┘
```

Every stunt produces content; content becomes a case study; case studies raise outbound
reply and close rates (hypothesis, tracked via reply-rate before/after first case study);
completed campaigns produce referrals (doc 06 §6: give $250 / get $250 on completed
campaigns only) and attract sellers, which deepens the catalog, which improves match
quality and proposal speed, which wins more campaigns. The operating metric that proves
the flywheel is turning: **repeat buyer rate** (D-009 metric #2).

## 4. Partnerships (supply + distribution, low-cost)

| Partner type | Offer to them | What we get | First 3 targets (to qualify, not yet contacted) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Event organizers (run clubs, trivia nights, markets, tournaments) | New sponsor revenue, zero sales work, 80% payout (D-005) | Repeatable inventory + built-in audiences | Denver run clubs, poker rooms, farmers markets |
| Venues (breweries, coffee shops, coworking) | Foot traffic + activation fees | Pre-cleared venue list (kills the biggest pre-production time sink, doc 07 §9) | RiNo breweries, LoDo cafés |
| Chambers / BIDs (Denver Metro Chamber, RiNo Art District, Downtown Denver Partnership) | Member benefit: turnkey local sponsorships | Trust + local-business distribution (D-004 segment 3) | Listed; outreach week 3 |
| Coworking (Industrious, local operators) | Community programming budget becomes sponsorable | Startup/DTC buyer density in one room | 2 spaces for a lunch-and-learn |

Rule: partnerships get a named owner and a next action in the CRM or they don't count.
No revenue-share complexity in month 1 — standard fees (D-005) apply; formal partner terms
are a post-$10k-GMV question.

## 5. What we deliberately do NOT do yet

- **Paid ads** (search/social): no case studies, no landing-page conversion data, AOV too
  low to buy cold traffic profitably. Revisit after 10 completed campaigns with CAC math.
- **Mass PR / PR agency:** nothing to show yet; one earned local story per real stunt beats
  a wire release. No press release before the first completed public case study.
- **App store / mobile app:** web only (D-011); the product is concierge + admin (D-001).
- **Self-serve marketplace opening:** gated by D-001 (≥10 completed paid campaigns, ≥3
  repeat buyers).
- **Second city:** gated by doc 20 criteria (D-018). No exceptions for exciting one-off leads —
  out-of-city inbound gets waitlisted or served as a fully-managed exception at ≥ $5k only
  if it requires zero new local infrastructure.
- **Subscriptions/auctions:** deferred per D-007.
- **Fake urgency, fake traction, purchased followers, undisclosed placements:** never (D-020,
  D-016 avoid-list).

   with Search Console after 60 days of category pages.

   outreach answers this; double down on the winner only.
4. Local press: does the poker stunt (gambling-adjacent) or the dog-walking day (wholesome)
   lead the pitch? Leaning dog-walking for tone, poker for novelty; decide when stunt #1 has
   a confirmed date.
