# 19 · Launch Stunt Engine

Stunts are paid campaigns that double as marketing (doc 17 channel 2). Every concept below
respects the avoid-list (D-016): **no trespass, vandalism, danger, harassment, impersonation,
deception, ambush marketing, disruption of others' events, unpermitted installs, fake
engagement, or undisclosed ads.** All participants are consenting adults, all placements are
disclosed, all venues/organizers give written permission.

Permit facts verified for planning (Denver):
- Film/photography permits on city property are **free** via Denver Public Event & Film
  Permitting (costs only if city staffing is required). Sources:
  [denvergov.org film permitting](https://denvergov.org/Government/Agencies-Departments-Offices/Agencies-Departments-Offices-Directory/Office-of-Special-Events/Film),
  [Arts & Venues PEFP](https://www.artsandvenuesdenver.com/programs/denver-public-event-film-permitting).
- Denver **park** event permits require applications ≥ 60 days out; the 2026 fee schedule
  shows special-event fees from ~$380 per 2-hour period. Sources:
  [2026 park permit application](https://denvergov.org/files/assets/public/v/1/parks-and-recreation/documents/2026-park-permit-app_9.25.25.pdf),
  [2026 park permit fees](https://www.denvergov.org/files/assets/public/v/2/parks-and-recreation/documents/rentals-amp-permits/2026_parkpermitfees.pdf).
  **Consequence:** this challenges A-16 (< 2 weeks, < $200) for park-based concepts — month-1
  stunts therefore use private venues, ordinary sidewalk/road use, and free film permits, and
  we verify the no-permit-needed determination in writing per activation (doc 07 §6).

## 3. Top-3 execution one-pagers

### 3.1 Poker-tournament sponsorship (flagship; = seed listing D-008)

- **Concept:** a brand sponsors Aaron, a real player, at a $200-entry Denver-area tournament:
  apparel logo, table-side content, recap video. Fixed fee; **no stake in winnings, no
  guaranteed impressions, disclosure on all content, branding only where the card room
  permits** (D-008 constraints verbatim).
- **Buyer:** DTC/startup wanting novelty content; agencies testing us; category fits: apparel,
  beverages (non-alcohol angle safest), fintech, local services. Gambling-averse brands are
  routed to stunts 2/3.
- **Price:** D-008 tiers — Basic placement $450 · Placement + content $750 · Exclusive
  activation $1,500 (buyer pays +5%; seller payout 80%; est. contribution ≈ $37/$83/$166).
- **Deliverables:** tiered — logo placement photos (all tiers); +10 photos, 60s+ vertical
  recap, 2 disclosed social posts (tier 2); +exclusive category, pre/post interview content,
  buyer logo on rail where venue allows (tier 3).
- **Permits/venue:** no city permit (private venue); **written venue approval is the gate**
  (A-12: unvalidated — ask 2–3 card rooms in week 1, before selling tier 3).
- **Timeline:** venue approval days 3–10 → sell days 5–15 → execute at next weekly
  tournament → proof within 48h.
- **Content plan:** phone-shot vertical video + stills to doc 07 §7 spec; "can you sponsor a
  poker player?" founder post; buyer gets raw + cut assets.
- **Measurement:** deliverables completed, asset count, buyer-side engagement on their posts
  (their analytics, shared voluntarily), rebooking intent. No invented reach (D-020).
- **Risks:** venue refuses branding (mitigation: multiple rooms, tier 1 apparel-only fallback);
  early exit from tournament (agreement covers minimum content regardless of results);
  gambling-adjacency brand safety (A-15 counsel item; fixed-fee structure documented).

### 3.2 Sponsor a Denver dog-walking day

- **Concept:** one Saturday, 30–50 opted-in owners walk dogs in {brand} bandanas + leash
  flags across one photogenic neighborhood (RiNo/Wash Park perimeter sidewalks), converging
  on a partner patio "Yappy Hour" finale. Ordinary sidewalk dog-walking — no assembly,
  no installs, no park-permit dependency; finale on private patio with venue permission.
- **Buyer:** pet brands, vets/insurers, local retail, any wholesome consumer brand;
  the anti-poker option for conservative buyers.
- **Price:** managed campaign $2,500 (D-006: ≥ $1,500, 35–50% GM). Est. costs: bandanas/
  flags ~$400, walker payouts ~$750 (25–50 walkers × $15–30 via package structure),
  content contractor ~$300 → ~$1,450 pass-through-ish, ~42% GM at $2,500 — within band.
- **Deliverables:** 50+ photos, 2 edited reels (15s/60s), ≥ 25 disclosed owner posts,
  usage rights, proof package.
- **Permits/venue:** written determination that sidewalk walking needs no permit (confirm
  with Denver PEFP — film permit free if we stage shots on city property, per sources above);
  patio = venue letter. Avoids the 60-day park-permit lead entirely.
- **Timeline:** 2 weeks — week 1 recruit walkers (dog-owner groups, partner vet/pet shops),
  sell the buyer; week 2 produce bandanas (5-day rush printable), execute Saturday.
- **Content plan:** golden-hour route, one drone-free hero shot location, owner UGC hashtag,
  founder behind-the-scenes thread.
- **Measurement:** #walks completed (checklist at start/finish points), posts published with
  disclosure, asset count, patio traffic per venue's own count if shared.
- **Risks:** weather (rain date in agreement); walker no-shows (over-recruit 30%, backup
  protocol doc 07 §10); dog incidents (no off-leash anything, waivers, no dog-to-dog staging).

### 3.3 100 branded bike commutes

- **Concept:** 20–30 opted-in Denver cyclists ride their **normal commutes** with branded
  panniers/frame flags until 100 disclosed commutes are logged in ~2 weeks; sold as one
  citywide package. Ordinary lawful road/bike-lane use; no route changes, no group rides,
  no installs.
- **Buyer:** mobility/outdoor/beverage/tech brands; sustainability-positioned DTC; agencies
  wanting a repeatable "distributed OOH on humans" story.
- **Price:** managed campaign $3,500. Est. costs: gear/printing ~$700, rider incentives
  ~$1,000 (100 × $10/commute), content ~$400 → ~$2,100, ~40% GM — within D-006 band.
- **Deliverables:** 100 logged commutes (photo + timestamp each), 30 curated photos, 1
  montage reel, ≥ 15 disclosed rider posts, usage rights.
- **Permits/venue:** none required for ordinary riding (determination logged); free Denver
  film permit if staging b-roll on city property (sources above).
- **Timeline:** 3 weeks — recruit via bike shops/commuter groups (wk 1), gear production
  (wk 2), ride window (wks 2–3), proof rolling daily.
- **Content plan:** daily counter graphic ("commute 37/100"), rider POV clips, map-styled
  recap (aggregate, privacy-safe — no individual route publishing).
- **Measurement:** the count IS the measurement — 100 verifiable, timestamped instances;
  strongest proof story of the three.
- **Risks:** **safety is the real one (scored 6):** riders ride exactly as they already do,
  no filming while riding (mount or stop), helmets required in agreement, no incentive for
  speed/distance; a crash during a sponsored commute is reputationally ours regardless of
  fault — insurance question escalated to legal review before sale. Slow logging (extend
  window, cap at 30 riders); logo visibility (test pannier print at 10 ft before production).
